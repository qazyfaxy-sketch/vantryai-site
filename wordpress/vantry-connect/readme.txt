Lets Vantry publish fact-checked answer pages and the code AI tools read to your site.

== Description ==
For Vantry AI Visibility clients. After you paste your connect code under Settings > Vantry, Vantry can:

* publish and update its own pages (marked "Vantry" in your Pages list)
* add structured data (schema) to your homepage
* serve /llms.txt, a plain summary AI tools read
* let AI search crawlers read your site via WordPress's robots.txt

It can't change anything else. Disconnect any time; removed pages go to the Trash.
