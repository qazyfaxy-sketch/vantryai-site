Do I own my domain?

Yes. Your custom domain belongs to you. We arrange the setup and provide the ownership and access details with your website.

What happens after year one?

Your first year of hosting and your custom domain are included. After year one, $50/year keeps both running. Optional Site Care is separate. The $0-down option requires 12 monthly payments of $49 before the annual renewal arrangement.

Can I edit my site?

Yes. You receive an editor login so you can change your text and photos. If you prefer us to handle small updates, Site Care is available for $29/month.

How long until it's live?

Your previews usually take about 20 seconds to generate, sometimes around 30 seconds. After you choose and pay, a real person reviews your site and completes the domain setup before launch. We confirm the launch timing with you.

What if I don't like it?

You can explore your previews without paying. You get two free preview requests per email, each with three styles. If none feels right, you don’t have to buy. Review the published refund terms before making a purchase.

Do you use AI?

Yes. AI helps us build your site fast, and a real person reviews every site before it goes live.

Does “Google-ready” mean guaranteed rankings?

No. We prepare the site’s basic search information and structure. Search rankings and customer enquiries are not guaranteed.
