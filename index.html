Questions

Straight answers.

Why is it only $200?

AI writes the first draft of your words and layout in seconds, so we don’t bill you for hours of design time. We keep it to one focused page, which is what most local service businesses need. A real person still checks every site before it goes live.

Do I own my domain?

Yes. Your custom domain belongs to you. We arrange the setup and provide the ownership and access details with your website.

What happens after year one?

Your domain and hosting are included for the first year. After that, it’s $50/year to keep both running. We email you before every renewal.

Can I edit my site?

Yes. Just email us what you want changed and we'll do it. Changes are free for the first 30 days, and Site Care covers small updates after that.

How long until it's live?

Your previews take about 20 seconds. After you pay, a real person checks your site and connects your domain, and we email you when it’s live.

What if I don't like it?

Then don’t buy. Previews are free, and you can make two per email. If you buy and change your mind before your site goes live, you get a full refund. See the Refund Policy.

Do you use AI?

Yes. AI helps us build your site fast, and a real person reviews every site before it goes live.

Does “Google-ready” mean guaranteed rankings?

No. We set up your site’s basic search information so Google can understand it. No one can honestly guarantee rankings or calls.
