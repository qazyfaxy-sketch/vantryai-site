Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Vantry AI

Who we are

Vantry AI ("Vantry AI," "we," "us") provides automated missed-call text-back service to home service businesses, including sending SMS text messages on behalf of those businesses to their own customers.

What information we collect

We collect phone numbers of individuals who call a business phone number enrolled in our service, along with the timestamp of the call and the content of any resulting text message exchange. We do not collect names, email addresses, or other personal details unless a customer voluntarily provides them in a text reply.

How we use this information

Phone numbers and message content are used solely to operate the missed-call text-back service: sending an automated text reply to a customer whose call went unanswered, and notifying the enrolled business owner of the missed call. Message logs may be retained briefly for service delivery and troubleshooting.

Our commitment

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Opting out

Any recipient can reply STOP to any message to immediately stop receiving further texts. Reply HELP for assistance.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to booking@vantryai.com.
