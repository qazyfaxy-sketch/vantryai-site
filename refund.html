Refund Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

Here's exactly how refunds and cancellations work for each plan, in plain terms.

Previews are always free. If you buy and change your mind before your site goes live, you get your money back.

One-time website ($199.99)

  • Before your site goes live: full refund. If we've already registered your domain, it stays yours and we keep up to $20 to cover its cost.
  • After your site goes live: we don't refund the build, but you get 30 days of launch support. If something on your site doesn't match what we promised, we'll fix it for free.

$0-down plan ($49/month)

  • Before your site goes live: cancel and we refund what you've paid, minus up to $20 if we've already registered your domain.
  • After your site goes live: this plan has a 12-month minimum. If you cancel early, the rest of the 12 payments are still due. Once all 12 are paid, your site continues at the $50/year renewal.

Site Care ($29/month)

Cancel anytime. Site Care stops at the end of your current month. We don't give refunds for partial months.

Yearly renewal ($50/year)

We email you before your renewal date. Cancel before then and you won't be charged. If you're charged and email us within 7 days, we'll refund the renewal and take the site offline. Your domain stays yours either way.

How to ask for a refund

Email booking@vantryai.com from the address you used to buy. Tell us your business name and what you'd like. We reply within 2 business days, and approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe. Banks usually take 5–10 business days to show it.

Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank. We can usually sort it out faster.
