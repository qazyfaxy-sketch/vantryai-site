Questions

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

No, and be careful with anyone who says they can. AI answers change and differ from person to person. What we promise is the work, a monthly report of exactly what changed, and your next month free if nothing new names you within 60 days.

How long until we see changes?

Often 30 to 60 days for the first new answers, because AI has to pick up the changes on the sites it reads. Your first fixes start in week 1.

Do I need to get on a call?

No. After you sign up you fill in a short form, about 5 minutes. Everything else happens by email.

How do you check what AI says?

We ask each AI your buyers' questions through its official service with web search turned on, and save the answers. The apps can answer a little differently for different people, so each report says when it was checked.

Which AI tools do you cover?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude, depending on your plan and what your buyers use.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are month to month. We email you 3 days before every renewal, and you can cancel in one click or by replying to any email.
